# Review one research result

This workflow does not authorize new model calls, messages, trading or publication.

1. State the question, target, information cutoff, baseline and decision.
2. Start from evidence and saved outputs. Check identity, definition, unit, period, availability and revision separately. Repeated sources are not independent corroboration.
3. Recompute a decisive result with independently expressed arithmetic. A hash proves matching bytes, not meaning.
4. Inspect relevant attempted variants. Keep failed reviews and losses visible. Do not present a favorable post-selected subgroup as an untouched test.
5. Separate model answers, deterministic calculations, reviewed templates and human judgments. Distinguish requested from verified served model identity; leave unknown costs unknown.
6. Ask the owner to reconstruct one meaningful step. Record only demonstrated understanding, distinguishing assistance from unaided work.
7. Produce a short report: question, method, baseline, result, strongest limitation, next discriminating test, reproduction commands and their limits.
8. Apply publication checks to the precise proposed files. Exclude private data and retain research-only authority.
